# Long REL in ACV toggles the 1 kHz filter.
source analysis/keys_setup.md section 2.2 REL long press (filter_1khz 0x200080B9 in modes 2 and 4); analysis/lcd_map.md section 4.2 (1 kHz w2b31)
source preconditions: rotary-memory bytes of the supplied calibration EEPROM image (0x08..0x0E = 01 03 06 0A 18 16 13)
dial v
wait 3
press MODE
wait 2
input V 1.0 ac freq=50
wait 3
long REL
wait 2
expect seg "1 kHz" on
long REL
wait 2
expect seg "1 kHz" off
