Reset all settings

Every setting of this program goes back to its default: folders, CSV format, time server, update check, diagnosis and the derived-value options. The meter's own settings and your files are not touched.

Format the SD card

Everything on the meter's card is erased: the log files and cal.bin, the backup of the calibration (make a new one afterwards: MEM while switching on, REL, MODE). The card becomes exFAT. This needs the two-part bootloader. Type FORMAT to confirm.

Formatting the SD card

Meter on, Bluetooth on (hold PEAK), phone app closed.
