# lcd_init's display update in the splash routine; the port then waits a 1.19 ms safety margin, DESIGN 7.1).  The bootloader's TIM2 keeps counting into the application (DESIGN 3.2
# step 1): v2.05 takes its update at 35.885 ms and the update event of its own TIM2 set-up at 36.971 ms, two ticks; the
